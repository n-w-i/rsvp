    Settings

    More words per flash suits familiar material; one word is best for focus.
    How much longer to hold on commas, full stops and paragraph ends.
    Captions, equations and bibliography entries aren't prose — flashing them word by word rarely helps. Inline citations like [13] are always removed.
    Backs up a few words when you unpause, so you re-enter with context.
